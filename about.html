Angel Ayala received his bachelor degree in computer engineering from the Universidad Central de Chile, Chile, in 2018. His Master's and Ph.D. degree in Computer Science was received from the Universidade de Pernambuco, Brasil, in 2021 and 2025, respectively. His research focused on learning-based methods for autonomous planning and perception under the object-goal navigation problem with a quadcopter. In 2025, was a visiting fellow withint the Autonomous Agents and Robotics Research (A2R2) group. He was awarded with the 3rd in the Master Thesis competition at the Congresso Brasileiro de Inteligência Computacional 2021 (CBIC), and a Runner-Up Best Poster PhD Student at 2025 Latin American Summer School on Robotics (LACORO). One of his most notable publications appeared in the journal Artificial Intelligence Review, entitled “UAV control in autonomous object-goal navigation: A systematic literature review.” Its current research areas are robotics, reinforcement learning, and computer vision methodologies, with a strong focus on multi-agent systems.
